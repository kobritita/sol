About Us

History:

SÒL was born in Valencia with a very simple idea: to reconnect with the land and everything it gives us. From that root, we work to create plant-based products with soul, combining tradition, sustainability and a modern outlook towards the future. The brand draws inspiration from the Valencian garden and the Mediterranean way of life, where nature, light and the land are part of our identity. We believe in a more conscious, healthy and environmentally respectful diet, and in recovering the balance between who we are and what we consume. The purpose is clear: to cultivate a greener, more local and more authentic future.

Mission, Vision and Values:

Mission

To promote responsible consumption that respects the land and nature, offering food that is good for people and for the planet.

Vision

To become a benchmark within the vegetarian and vegan food sector, promoting a more sustainable lifestyle connected to Mediterranean culture.

Values

Sustainability, respect for nature, responsible innovation, quality and commitment to the community and the territory.

How we work:

At SÒL we work closely with local producers from the Comunitat Valenciana, selecting seasonal and local ingredients. Each product goes through a careful production process, where quality and respect for the environment are always the priority. We believe in transparency: we know where our food comes from and how it has been grown. That is how we understand food: honest, close and rooted.
