Cookie Policy

In compliance with current regulations on data protection and information society services, SÒL informs users about the use of cookies on this website.

Cookies are small text files that are stored on the user's device when accessing certain web pages. Their purpose is to recognise the user, facilitate navigation, ensure the correct functioning of the website and, in some cases, collect statistical information about its use.

This website uses the following categories of cookies:

  • Technical cookies: necessary for the functioning of the website and the provision of the services offered.
  • Personalisation cookies: allow the user's preferences to be remembered, such as language or settings.
  • Analytics cookies: allow the number of users to be quantified and statistical analyses of website usage to be carried out.
  • Advertising cookies: allow advertising spaces to be managed based on user behaviour.

The use of non-essential cookies will be subject to the user's prior consent, who may accept, reject or configure them at any time through the configuration panel or from their browser.

The user may also allow, block or delete cookies installed on their device through their browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect the correct functioning of the website.

SÒL may update this Cookie Policy to adapt it to legislative or technical changes. Users are advised to review this policy periodically.

For more information on the processing of personal data, users may consult our Privacy Policy.
