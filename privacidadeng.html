SÒL Privacy Policy

1. Data Controller

In compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Organic Law 3/2018 on Personal Data Protection and guarantee of digital rights (LOPDGDD), users are informed that the data controller responsible for processing the personal data collected through this website is SÒL, with registered address at Carrer de la Pau, 12 – 46002 València, and contact email: hola@sol-valencia.com.

2. Data Collected

Through the forms available on this website, the following personal data may be collected: name, email address, phone number, and any other information that the user voluntarily provides in the message or description field.

3. Purpose of Processing

The data provided by the user will be processed for the following purposes: responding to inquiries, requests, or applications submitted through the contact, wholesale, restaurant, or catering forms; managing the commercial relationship with users interested in SÒL services; and sending information related to the requested services.

4. Legal Basis

The legal basis for processing the data is the user’s consent, freely and unequivocally given when completing and submitting the forms available on the website, as well as SÒL’s legitimate interest in responding to received requests.

5. Data Retention

Personal data will be retained for the time necessary to respond to the user’s request and, subsequently, for the legally established periods required to address any liabilities arising from the processing.

6. Recipients

Personal data will not be shared with third parties except where legally required. SÒL does not carry out international data transfers.

7. User Rights

Users have the right to access, rectify, erase, or object to the processing of their personal data, request restriction of processing, and, where applicable, exercise the right to data portability. To exercise these rights, users may contact SÒL by email at hola@sol-valencia.com, indicating the right they wish to exercise and providing a copy of their identification document.

Likewise, users have the right to file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) if they believe that the processing of their data does not comply with current regulations.

8. Security

SÒL adopts the necessary technical and organizational measures to guarantee the security of personal data and prevent its alteration, loss, unauthorized processing, or access.

9. Updates

SÒL reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy to adapt it to legislative or technical changes. Users are advised to review it periodically.
